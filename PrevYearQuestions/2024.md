![Transformer Excitation and Induced Voltage Phenomena](diagrams/2024_q1b_transformer.png)

> [!NOTE] **Diagrammatic Details for Text-Only AI & Analytical Understanding**
> - **Magnetic Core:** A rectangular closed ferromagnetic core consisting of two vertical limbs (left and right) linked by top and bottom yokes.
> - **Mutual Flux ($\Phi_{\text{mutual}}$):** A closed dashed rectangular path inside the core labeled **"$\Phi\text{ mutual}$"** with four nodal points at the corners, illustrating the common magnetic flux that couples both windings.
> - **Primary Circuit (Left Limb — "coil1"):**
>   - Wound with **$N_1$ turns** around the left core leg.
>   - Excited by an electrical source $v$ (illustrated with DC battery polarity: $+$ terminal at the top, $-$ terminal at the bottom) via an inline single-pole switch on the upper conductor.
>   - The instantaneous primary current is denoted as $i_1$, directed into the upper winding terminal ($\rightarrow i_1$).
>   - The induced counter-electromotive force in coil 1 is designated as $e_1$, indicated by an upward vertical arrow ($\uparrow e_1$) across the coil.
> - **Secondary Circuit (Right Limb — "coil2"):**
>   - Wound with **$N_2$ turns** around the right core leg.
>   - The terminals connect across a passive resistive load element ($R$).
>   - The induced secondary electromotive force is designated as $e_2$, indicated by an upward vertical arrow ($\uparrow e_2$) across the coil.
>   - The secondary current $i_2$ flows out from the upper terminal into the resistive load as indicated by an arrow ($\rightarrow i_2$).
> - **Phenomena Addressed:**
>   - Time-varying flux establishment upon closing the switch ($v \rightarrow i_1(t) \rightarrow \Phi(t)$).
>   - Self-induction in coil 1 according to Faraday's Law and Lenz's Law: $e_1 = -N_1 \frac{d\Phi}{dt}$.
>   - Mutual induction in coil 2 linking the mutual flux: $e_2 = -N_2 \frac{d\Phi}{dt}$.
>   - Direction of secondary induced emf, current $i_2$, and the secondary reaction flux opposing the rate of change of core flux.
